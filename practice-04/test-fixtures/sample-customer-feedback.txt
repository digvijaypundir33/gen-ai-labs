Customer Feedback Submission
Product: Google Support Portal
Rating: 4/5

I wanted to share some thoughts after using the support portal for about six weeks.
Overall the experience has been solid. Ticket submission is quick and the priority
field actually seems to matter - my critical issue got a response within ten minutes,
which honestly surprised me given how bad support usually is elsewhere.

The live chat feature is nice for quick questions, though it occasionally gives a
generic answer when my question is very specific to our setup. The one thing I would
genuinely like improved is the search in the knowledge base - I often know the article
I want exists somewhere but can't find it by searching, and end up just opening a
ticket instead. If that got fixed, I probably wouldn't need to contact support nearly
as often. Otherwise, keep doing what you are doing, this has been a noticeable
improvement over the old system.
